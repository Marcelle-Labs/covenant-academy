## Source authority

This script is drafted under two governing documents, which remain the
authority. They are referenced here rather than reproduced — the research ledger
and its textual-witness findings stay in Linear, and this file stays readable
editorial source.

- **Miriam at the Nile — Authority Pack v1** (Linear, Academy project) —
  governs every factual claim, the claim classes, the KJV modernization notes,
  the permitted historical reconstruction, the later-tradition quarantine, and
  the developmental gate on Pharaoh's decree.
- **Academy Child Companion — Character Canon v2** (Linear, Academy project) —
  governs Nia. Her repository-side canon is `canon/nia.md`, cited by her entry
  in `canon/cast.json`. Canon v1 is superseded and is not the authority.

**Primary source:** Exodus 1:15–2:10. **English baseline:** KJV, modernized in
spoken delivery where archaic wording would confuse the listener.

Two authority-pack rulings do visible work in the text below and should survive
any revision:

1. **Exodus does not name the sister in this scene.** The identification as
   Miriam is a strong canonical inference from later references. Beat 1 explains
   this once, and the script thereafter calls her Miriam while preserving the
   distinction. Beat 6 returns to it.
2. **No age is assigned to her.** Nia guesses; the answer is that the story does
   not tell us. That exchange is the rule being taught, not a gap to be filled.

Birds calling.
