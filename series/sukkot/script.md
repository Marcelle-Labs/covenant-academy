## Beat 1 — A long walk

A long, long time ago — longer ago than Abba, longer ago than Abba's abba, longer than anyone you have ever met — there was a family. A really big family. So many people that if they all stood in a line, you could not see the end of it. And this whole family was walking. Not walking to the park. Not walking out to the car. They were walking to a new home. A home they had never seen before. Nobody in the family had seen it yet. They just knew that the King was taking them there. So they walked.

## Beat 2 — They lived in tents

Now. When you go on a long trip, where do you sleep? In a bed. In a house. With a roof over your head. This family did not have houses. They had tents. Every single one of them slept in a tent. Cloth walls. A cloth roof. When the wind blew, the walls moved. When it rained, you could hear every single drop, right above your head. It was not a strong house. It was a tent. And that is where they lived. Not for one night. Not for one week.

## Beat 3 — Up and down and up again

And here is the part I want you to picture. Every so often, it was time to move again. So the whole family took their tents down. All of them. They rolled up the cloth. They pulled up the poles. They picked up everything they owned, and they carried it. And they walked. And when they stopped, they put all the tents back up again. And then, after a while — down came the tents, and they walked again. Up. Down. Up. Down. Over, and over, and over.

## Beat 4 — Forty years

Do you know how long they did that? Forty years. Forty. You are six. Six years — that is your whole life. Every single birthday you have ever had. Now imagine your whole life again. And again. You would have to do that six whole times, and then a little bit more, before you got to forty years. Children were born in those tents. They learned to walk in those tents. They grew all the way up in those tents. And they got married, and they had their own children — in tents. For most of that family, the tent was not something that happened on the way to their life. The tent was their life.

## Beat 5 — Why we sit outside

So. This week, we are going to go outside and sit in a little booth with branches on the roof. And when we do, somebody might ask: why are we sitting out here, when we have a perfectly good house right there? This is why. Because a long time ago, our family lived in tents. And they were told: do not forget this. Tell your children. And tell your children's children. So that is what Abba is doing right now. I am telling you. That is the whole reason. We sit outside for one week, so that we remember.
