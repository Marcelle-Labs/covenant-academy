## 0. Calendation

Settled. The household keeps the **Enoch/Zadok 364-day calendar**, Wednesday-anchor, continuous-week reconstruction. Full ruling and sources on COV-30; Sabbath consequence on COV-62.

The seventh month, 2026:

| | Enoch date | Gregorian |
|---|---|---|
| Trumpets | 7/1 | Wed Sep 23 |
| Atonement | 7/10 | Fri Oct 2 |
| **Tabernacles** | **7/15–21** | **Wed Oct 7 – Tue Oct 13** |
| Eighth Day | 7/22 | Wed Oct 14 |

Days run sunset to sunset. The card's first track lands the evening of **Tue Oct 6**.

**Three non-work days fall inside the window:**

- **Wed Oct 7** — 7/15, holy convocation (Lev 23:35)
- **Sat Oct 10** — 7/18, weekly Sabbath
- **Wed Oct 14** — 7/22, holy convocation (Lev 23:36)

Two consequences the tracks have to respect:

1. **The sukkah is built before the feast begins** — complete before sunset Tue Oct 6. It cannot be a day-one activity, because day one is a convocation. This resolves the T6 timing question that was open (see §4).
2. **T11 is datable.** "One more day" is 7/22, Wed Oct 14 — the eighth day, and the card's last full day.

Ordinal month numbering is deliberate. *Tishrei* is a Babylonian month name; Enoch and Jubilees number months, and the spec should say what the household keeps.

**Still open:** track-to-day assignment. Twelve tracks across eight days with three convocation days is not a flat distribution, and which track lands on which evening is an editorial call, not a calendrical one. Not blocking script review.

## 2. Track spec

Each track: intent, form, duration, theme, and the two readings. **Surface** is what Quinn gets — complete, nothing missing. **Depth** is what Samantha gets from the *same words*. Never a separate line, never a wink.

---

**T1 — "It's Sukkot"** · welcome · 2 min · *dwelling*
**Intent:** Name the week. Set the voice. Establish this is a happy card.
**Surface:** Something special starts tonight and it lasts a whole week.
**Depth:** Time is being marked by someone else's calendar, not the culture's.
**Note:** Lands the evening of Tue Oct 6, as 7/15 begins. The spoken date is now settled and recordable.

**T2 — "The King wants to be with His family"** · story · 4 min · *dwelling*
**Intent:** State the through-line plainly, once, so every other track hangs on it.
**Surface:** The King loves His family and doesn't want to be far away.
**Depth:** The whole covenant arc in one sentence — the thing all six feasts are variations on.

**T3 — "The people who lived in tents"** · story · 5 min · *provision, remembering*
**Intent:** The wilderness, concretely. Tents, walking, a long time.
**Surface:** A long time ago the family lived in tents while they walked to a new home.
**Depth:** Formation happens in transit, not on arrival. Forty years is most of a lifetime.

**T4 — "And He put His tent right in the middle"** · story · 4 min · *dwelling* ← **the hinge of the arc**
**Intent:** The single image the whole card exists to deliver.
**Surface:** The King didn't want them to be alone out there, so He came and stayed in a tent too — right in the middle, where everyone could see it.
**Depth:** Incarnation, stated without the word. *Tabernacled among us.* The King accepts the same accommodations as His people.
**Note:** Understatement is the technique. Do not explain this track. The pause after it does the work.

**T5 — "Food in the morning"** · story · 4 min · *provision*
**Intent:** Daily provision, enough-for-today.
**Surface:** Every single morning there was food on the ground. Every morning. Even when they forgot to say thank you.
**Depth:** Give us this day. Provision that can't be hoarded is provision that has to be trusted.

**T6 — "Let's build one"** · do-together prompt · 3 min · *shelter*
**Intent:** Kinesthetic. Card points at a real activity with real hands.
**Surface:** Go out to the fort with Abba. Branches on top. Ask to sleep in it.
**Depth:** The commandment is performed, not studied. The body learns first.
**Note:** **Timing resolved.** The sukkah is standing before the card is ever played — it goes up before sunset Tue Oct 6, because day one is a convocation. So this track points at a booth that already exists rather than sending her to build one. Rewritten from "go build a fort" to "go out to the fort." If the build is to be recorded as part of the arc, that's a separate pre-feast track and is not in this twelve.

**T7 — "You can see the stars through the roof"** · story/wonder · 4 min · *shelter, flimsiness*
**Intent:** Why is it deliberately weak?
**Surface:** It's not a strong house. It's a little wobbly. You can see stars through the roof — and that's on purpose.
**Depth:** Chosen precariousness as a spiritual discipline. Safety was never located in the structure.

**T8 — Song: "Come and stay"** · song · 3 min · *dwelling*
**Intent:** The retention vector. Short, repetitive, singable by week's end.
**Surface:** A song about the King coming to stay.
**Depth:** —
**Note:** `[OPEN]` Melody doesn't exist. Suno for auditioning only; final take is family voice. The audition artifact must not reach a bundle — no mechanism enforces this yet.

**T9 — "He said: be happy"** · story · 4 min · *commanded joy*
**Intent:** The most surprising thing in the text and the easiest to land at six.
**Surface:** The King didn't just say "remember." He said "be happy." Being happy is part of doing it right.
**Depth:** Joy as obedience, not as mood. The hardest instruction in the Torah for an adult to actually follow.

**T10 — "Who else can come?"** · story · 4 min · *welcoming guests*
**Intent:** The booth is open. Belonging extends outward.
**Surface:** A sukkah always has room. Who should we invite?
**Depth:** The table is the theology. Belonging that doesn't extend isn't belonging, it's a club.

**T11 — "One more day"** · story · 4 min · *one more day* ← **the emotional close**
**Intent:** Shemini Atzeret. The week ends and He asks for one more.
**Surface:** When the week was over, the King said: stay one more day. He wasn't ready for it to be over.
**Depth:** God as the one reluctant to end the visit. Reverses the intuition that we pursue and He tolerates.
**Note:** 7/22 — Wed Oct 14. A convocation day, and the last full day of the arc.

**T12 — "Go tell Abba"** · handoff · 2 min · —
**Intent:** Assessment via narration. The card prompts; the parent receives.
**Surface:** Go find Abba and tell him your favorite part.
**Depth:** —
**Note:** This is the whole assessment instrument. No quiz, no failure state.
