## Formation target

pay attention — notice, ask, try, and remember.

The feast-spine refrain "The King wants to be with His family" (COV-83) is

## Beats

### Beat 1 — First day

*[warm, close, unhurried; father-to-daughter, not school-announcer energy]*

Quinn, good morning. Today's the first day. And there's one thing I want you to know before you start. You're going to learn a lot this year. Numbers. Words. Stories. Things people figured out a long time ago. Things growing outside right now. Sometimes somebody is going to ask you a question and you'll know the answer. And sometimes you won't. That's okay.

### Beat 2 — The expectation

*[small pause after "That's okay" before starting this beat]*

You don't have to already know everything. Your job is to notice. Notice something you didn't see before. Ask when something doesn't make sense. Try when you're not sure. Remember something you learned yesterday. That's what I want from you. Not to know everything. To pay attention. And my job is to help you.

### Beat 3 — The first mission

So I have one mission for you today. Sometime before tonight, find one thing you usually walk right past. Maybe it's a sound. Maybe it's a bug. Maybe it's a word. Maybe it's something somebody in our family does every day. Stop for a second. Look at it. Wonder about it. You don't even have to know what it means yet. Just notice it.

### Beat 4 — Retrieval promise

And tonight, I'll ask you what you found. That's your first job.

*[gentle smile in the voice]*

Go notice something, baby.
